##About-Me

I'm a hands-on technologist with a strong interest in **cybersecurity,
offensive security, Linux systems, self-hosted infrastructure, and
practical automation**. I enjoy taking systems apart conceptually,
understanding how their pieces interact, and building reliable solutions
from what I learn.

My experience spans Linux and Windows environments, Docker-based
deployments, networking, server and NAS administration, API-driven
applications, and locally hosted AI systems. I learn best by building,
troubleshooting, documenting, and testing in practice---not by stopping
at theory.

I'm currently developing my skills along an **ethical hacking and
red-team-oriented path**, with an emphasis on reconnaissance,
enumeration, understanding attack chains, documenting findings, and
improving technical depth through labs and hands-on exercises.

Outside of technology, I bring years of leadership, training, customer
service, and operational responsibility from pharmacy work. That
background has strengthened my attention to detail, communication,
process discipline, and ability to stay composed when accuracy matters.

> **My approach:** learn the fundamentals, test carefully, document
> clearly, and keep improving.
